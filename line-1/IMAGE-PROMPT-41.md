# Round 41 image direction

The generated reference motif was a compact, worn charcoal cord holding three
uneven red-ochre, yellow-ochre and chalk-white pebbles on warm cave rock. It
contained no text, numbers, person, hand, logo, border or watermark. Only that
earth-pigment idea was added to unused lower-left rock: the finalized wall
keeps every ancestor pixel outside the 130 by 110-pixel addition rectangle.

The inherited central Pepe remains the wall's main cave figure. His two hands
were retained unchanged, each with four fingers and one thumb. The composition
has no newly painted hands or stencils.
